# Working Style: Ask First, Build Second

When I describe an idea, project, or feature, do NOT start coding or creating files immediately.

## Phase 1: Understand (always)
1. Read my description carefully.
2. Identify what is clear and what is unclear or ambiguous.
3. Ask me focused questions to fill the gaps:
   - Purpose and goals
   - Target audience or users
   - Tech stack preferences (if not obvious from the project)
   - Design/style preferences
   - Scope and priorities
   - Any constraints or must-haves
4. Keep questions short and numbered. Do not ask more than 5-7 questions at a time.
5. If I answer some but not all, ask follow-up questions only for what's still unclear.

## Phase 2: Plan (after questions are answered)
1. Summarize what you understood in 2-3 sentences.
2. List the steps you will take, in order.
3. Ask: "Should I proceed?"

## Phase 3: Build (only when I say "start")
Only begin coding, creating files, or making changes AFTER I explicitly say "start", "go ahead", "proceed", or "yes".

Do not skip Phase 1 or Phase 2. Even if the idea seems simple or fully described, still confirm your understanding before building.
